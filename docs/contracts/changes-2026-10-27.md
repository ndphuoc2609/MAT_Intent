## Missing inventory fact: keep the request in draft handoff

Six cases expected a customer-facing send even though the fixture had no
verified inventory fact for the requested vehicle/color/variant. The engine
returns `HANDOFF_DRAFT_ONLY`; it does not state that inventory is available.

## Out-of-scope and unknown scope: route to scope review

`spam_loan_ad`, `scope_unknown`, and `err_injection` previously expected
`REVIEW_UNRESOLVED`. Their semantics explicitly classify the input as
out-of-scope or unknown, so the engine applies the scope gate before acting on
the proposed `UNRESOLVED` operation. The expected route is now `REVIEW_SCOPE`;
the old expectation is recorded here.

## Unsupported list-price slot: ask which price the customer wants

Six synthetic semantics asserted `price_kind=list` for comments that only said
“giá” and did not say “niêm yết”. Those fixtures now omit the unsupported slot
and expect `WAITING_USER` plus `ask_clarification` for price kind. Mixed
complaint cases still hand off the complaint while asking the price question.
This corrects the semantic fixture and expected policy outcome; it does not
weaken slot evidence or turn these cases into synthetic human gold.

Cases: `complaint_plus_price`, `neg_not_tucson`, `pref_inbox_ok`,
`multi_user_slots`, `conditional_next_month`, and
`mixed_pref_price_complaint`.

## Inherited vehicle provenance in fixtures

Seventeen v1 fixture slots that used the sole vehicle listed in the postcard
without naming it in the current comment are now marked `explicit=false` and
carry a slot evidence excerpt. The engine fills the source post ID, page actor,
and state snapshot before validation. Expected business outcomes were not
changed by these annotations.
